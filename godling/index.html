What it is

Your Blub hatches in a quiet meadow by a little village. Send it off on trips and it comes home with a journal of what it got up to: it saved a lamb, it ate the baker's pie, it stared down a bear. You read each story and tell it "Good Blub!" or "No!"

It learns from you. Over days and weeks it grows into a gentle saint or a dreaded beast, bold or timid, a grazer or a hunter, sprouting horns or wings or moss along the way. The villagers watch, and come to love it, or fear it.

In the meadow

Stories that shape it

Hundreds of little trip stories, tough calls with no easy answer, and story chains that remember what you chose.

A village with a mood

Villagers gather wood, build things, ask for favors, leave offerings, and decide what kind of god you are.

Build and farm

Place fences, gardens and wells. Plant seeds your Blub brings home, and feed the harvest to it or the village.

Grow, mutate, pass on

Mutations, legends, and generations: a legend lays an egg, and its baby inherits a little of who it was.

Friends and rivals

Swap visit codes so friends' Blubs come to stay, battle each other, and see off the rival village's champion.

Seasons and holidays

Autumn leaves, a frozen pond, spring blossoms, Halloween costumes, and a party on your Blub's hatch day.
