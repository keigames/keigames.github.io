Help test Godling

Godling is almost ready for Google Play. Before it can launch, Google needs a group of real players to try it for two weeks. It's free, and joining takes about two minutes.

Join the tester group

Sign in with the same Google account as the Play Store on your phone, then tap "Join group". Nobody sees your email but us.

Opened this from Instagram or another app? Its built-in browser isn't signed in to Google, so the group looks locked. Tap ··· and choose Open in browser first.

Become a tester

Open this on your Android phone and tap "Become a tester". If it says the test isn't available yet, Google is still reviewing it: try again in a day or two.

Install and play

Then grab it from the Play Store like any other app. Updates arrive on their own.

Good to know

  • Keep it installed for at least two weeks and open it now and then. Google counts testers day by day, and the launch depends on it.
  • Testers get the full game free. Look for the "Full game keys" post in the tester group, take one key, and redeem it in the Play Store.
  • Tell us everything: bugs, confusing bits, and the parts you love. Email godling.dev@gmail.com.
  • On iPhone? An iPhone test is coming later. We'll let the group know.
  • Godling has no ads and collects no personal data. Privacy policy.
