Godling Privacy Policy
Effective October 28, 2026

Godling is made by Kei Games ("we"). The short version: Godling does not collect, store, or share any personal information about you. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking.

What Godling keeps, and where

  • Your game save (your Blub, your village, your choices) is stored only on your device. It is never sent to us or anyone else. Uninstalling Godling deletes it. (Your phone's own backups, such as iCloud, may include it, under Apple's or Google's policies.)
  • Settings, such as sound and notifications, are stored on your device in the same way.

What Godling does not do

  • It does not collect your name, email, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers, or advertising ID.
  • It does not show ads and contains no advertising or analytics code.
  • It does not need an account, and it does not need the internet to play.
  • We have no servers that receive any data from the game.

Purchases

Godling offers a one-time purchase to unlock the full game. Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play (on Android) or the App Store (on iPhone and iPad), under their own privacy policies. We do not receive your payment details. The game only learns whether the full game has been unlocked on your account, so it can unlock it on your device.

Notifications

If you allow them, Godling schedules notifications on your device (for example, when your Blub comes home from a trip). They are created by the game on your device; nothing is sent from a server. You can turn them off in the game's settings or your phone's settings at any time.

Visit codes

Godling lets you share a "visit code" or QR code with friends so their Blub can visit. The code is made on your device and only contains your Blub's name, how it looks, its personality, your village's mood, and a random ID made by the game. It is only shared if you choose to share it, and only with the people you send it to. We never see it.

When you open "Have a visitor", Godling checks your clipboard for a visit code you've copied, to fill it in for you. Nothing from your clipboard leaves your device.

Children

Godling is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, we'll update this page and the date above. If Godling ever starts collecting any data, we'll say so here first.
